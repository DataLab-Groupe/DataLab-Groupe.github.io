# Beyond the Cool Side: The Dark Side of AI Implementation
While the industry focuses on the exciting potential of LLMs and AI Agents, DataLab Groupe addressed a critical reality: **these technologies often represent less than 10% of the actual costs and timelines for industrial-scale solutions.**

At a time when the AI community has "stars in their eyes" for Agentic AI, our team provided an unfiltered perspective on the **challenging, steep, sometimes frustrating, but always motivating journey** of deploying GenAI solutions on an industrial scale.

# Masterclass: Enterprise RAG - The Industrial Approach
**November 26th, 2:10 PM - 2:40 PM | Grand Palais (Masterclass South)**
Florence BAUPREY, Aldrick ZAPPELLINI and Aymen SHABOU delivered a masterclass: **"Enterprise RAG: Industrial Approach for Balancing Performance, Security, Scalability and Digital Sobriety"**
Key Topics covered:
* Real-world challenges in RAG deployment at enterprise scale
* Balancing AI performance with security requirements
* Strategies for sustainable and scalable AI implementation
* Digital sobriety in AI architecture design

# The Reality of Industrial AI Deployment
Our presentation emphasized that **magic doesn't exist, even if many are selling it**. At DataLab Groupe, we believe in **consistency in our ideas** and transparency about the true challenges of scaling AI solutions in enterprise environments.
This event provided a unique opportunity to share our field experience and demonstrate how Crédit Agricole Group approaches AI deployment with both ambition and pragmatism, ensuring sustainable innovation that serves our customers and stakeholders.
